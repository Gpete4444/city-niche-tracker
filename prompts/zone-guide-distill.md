# Zone guide distill

Turn the operator's source into a short zone brief. The source may be a chat transcript. Keep the decisions. Drop the conversation.

## Output

Reply with markdown only. Start with `# Zone brief`. Use these headings when the source supports them. Omit a heading you cannot fill. Do not add a sales pitch, a contractor script, national rank tables, keyword difficulty, or cost-per-click figures.

```markdown
# Zone brief

## Market

## Cities

## Homepage

## Services

## Terminology

## Local angles

## Symptom themes
```

Rules:

- Cities: lines that start with `Prefer:` or `Skip:`, one city per line, with a short reason.
- Homepage: the niche the domain and homepage H1 stay on, and what must stay off the homepage H1.
- Services: a primary list (homepage cards first) and a secondary list (real service pages after the primary list). Name offers to avoid as page names. Stay inside 12 residential names.
- Terminology: words to use and words to avoid leading with.
- Local angles: climate, soil, and construction cues. Numbers are operator research, not facts to print as local statistics.
- Symptom themes: homeowner questions. These are FAQ and section angles, not new URLs.
- Do not invent cities, services, or statistics that are not in the source.
