Elizabeth Basement Finishing Pros finishes unfinished basements. The job is framing, drywall, ceilings, and floors that turn storage space into a family room, an office, a bathroom, or a bedroom.

The houses in Elizabeth are often early-to-mid 20th century. Their basements were built as storage: concrete floors and block walls. Finishing that space means measuring it, building the walls, and leaving the window where it is.

## How we work

A site visit comes first. A finisher and the homeowner stand in the unfinished basement and decide which rooms to build. The scope is written down before framing starts: which rooms, what they cost, and how long they take.

Elizabeth Basement Finishing Pros serves Elizabeth, NJ along with Colonia, Avenel, Iselin, Woodbridge, and Carteret.
