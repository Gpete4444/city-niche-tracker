## Egress Window Installation FAQs for Bellevue

**Do older Bellevue homes usually need a new foundation opening?**
Many do. Homes built before modern egress code often have a small basement window that doesn't meet current opening-size or sill-height minimums, which usually means cutting a new opening rather than just swapping the window.

**What does egress window installation cost in Bellevue?**
Costs typically range from around $2,500 for a well swap on an existing opening up to $9,000 or more for a full foundation cut with a new window and well. The exact number depends on wall thickness, well material, and how much drainage work is needed.

**How does Sarpy County permitting affect my timeline?**
If your project needs a permit — which is common for new foundation openings or basement bedroom conversions — approval typically adds 1–2 weeks before cutting can start. Work itself usually takes 1–2 days for the cut and window, plus about a day for the well and drainage.
