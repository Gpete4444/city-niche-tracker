## Egress Window Installation FAQs for Omaha

**Does my basement bedroom in Omaha need an egress window?**
If the room is used as a bedroom, it generally needs a compliant egress opening under the residential code Omaha has adopted. Many older Omaha homes have a small basement window in what's now a bedroom, which typically doesn't meet current minimums for opening size or sill height.

**How long does a typical Omaha egress project take from estimate to inspection?**
A site visit and written estimate usually happen within the same visit. If a permit is required, approval commonly takes 1–2 weeks, with the actual cutting and window install taking 1–2 days and the well and drainage work adding roughly another day.

**What does Omaha's clay soil mean for my window well?**
Clay soil drains slowly, so a well without a proper gravel base can hold water after heavy rain instead of letting it soak away. We layer gravel beneath and around the well and grade the surrounding soil to keep water moving away from the foundation.

**Is a permit always required for egress work in Omaha?**
Not always — cutting a new foundation opening or enlarging an existing one typically requires a permit, while a like-for-like window swap on an opening that isn't being changed usually doesn't. Requirements can vary by exact scope, so we confirm with the local building department before work starts.
