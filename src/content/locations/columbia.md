## Cabinet Refinishing & Remodeling FAQs for Columbia

Do older Columbia homes need a different refinishing approach?
Many older homes near the city center have solid-wood cabinets that respond well to standard refinishing, while newer construction on the outskirts sometimes uses veneer or laminate that may be better suited to refacing — we assess this at the free consultation.

How does Columbia's climate affect cabinet finish choices?
The humidity along the Congaree River corridor makes moisture-resistant primers and catalyzed conversion varnish a better long-term choice than standard lacquer, which is more prone to cracking as cabinets expand and contract seasonally.

What does cabinet refinishing typically cost in Columbia?
Most Columbia-area refinishing projects run $2,500–$6,000, with refacing running $6,000–$12,000 and full remodels starting around $12,000, depending on kitchen size and scope.

Do I need a permit for cabinet work in Columbia?
Cosmetic refinishing, refacing, and hardware upgrades typically don't require a permit in Columbia; full remodels involving plumbing, electrical, or structural changes usually do. Confirm specifics with the City of Columbia's building department.
