## Cabinet Refinishing & Remodeling FAQs for Columbia

**Does older housing in Columbia need more prep work?**
Homes with original wood cabinetry from older neighborhoods often need more sanding, repair, and priming than newer builder-grade boxes, since the finish and wood condition vary more. That's assessed during the in-home consultation before pricing.

**How does Columbia's climate affect the finish?**
Humid summers in the Midlands can slow cure time and cause lower-grade finishes to stay tacky or yellow. Company Brand Name uses cabinet-grade primers and conversion-varnish topcoats built to cure properly in that humidity.

**Do I need a permit for cabinet work in Columbia?**
Like-for-like refinishing or refacing typically doesn't require a permit. Projects with layout changes, new electrical, or plumbing relocation usually do — confirm specifics with the local building department.

**What does cabinet refinishing typically cost in Columbia?**
Most refinishing projects run $2,500–$5,500 for a standard kitchen, while refacing runs $6,000–$12,000 and a full remodel or custom built-in project can run $10,000–$25,000 or more.
