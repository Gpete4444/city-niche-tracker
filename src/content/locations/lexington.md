## Cabinet Refinishing & Remodeling FAQs for Lexington

**Do new-construction cabinets in Lexington need different prep?**
Laminate and thermofoil boxes common in newer Lexington homes need a bonding primer before a new topcoat, since standard paint won't hold on those surfaces long-term.

**Can I get a custom color instead of my builder-grade finish?**
Custom cabinet painting and staining is available for Lexington kitchens that want a color or stain beyond the standard builder options, using the same spray equipment as full refinishing jobs.

**What does a refacing project cost in Lexington?**
A standard 10x10 kitchen refacing project — new doors and drawer fronts with existing boxes refinished — typically runs $6,000–$12,000 depending on door style and hardware.

**Do I need a permit for cabinet work in Lexington?**
Like-for-like refinishing or refacing usually doesn't require one. Structural layout changes, new electrical, or plumbing relocation typically do — confirm with the Town of Lexington building department.
