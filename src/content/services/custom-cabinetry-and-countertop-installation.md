## Custom Cabinetry & Countertop Installation FAQs

What's the difference between stock and custom cabinetry?
Stock cabinetry comes in standard sizes and finishes with shorter lead times; custom cabinetry is built to your exact specifications but takes longer to fabricate.

How do I choose a countertop material?
It depends on your priorities — engineered stone offers low maintenance and durability, while natural stone offers unique veining but needs more care.

Can I install new countertops without replacing cabinetry?
Yes, as long as your existing cabinetry is structurally sound and the new countertop fits the footprint.

## DIY vs. Professional Custom Cabinetry & Countertop Installation

Minor tasks like updating cabinet hardware are reasonable DIY projects. Cabinetry installation and countertop templating/fabrication require precision and specialized tools — professional installation avoids costly measurement errors and ensures a proper fit.

## When to Call a Pro for Custom Cabinetry & Countertop Installation
