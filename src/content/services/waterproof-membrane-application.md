Basement bathroom finishing builds a small bath into an unfinished corner. The photos show that corner marked, framed, drywalled, floored, and then fitted with a simple vanity, a mirror, a towel bar, and a light.

Elizabeth Basement Finishing Pros keeps the bath modest. It is a vanity and a finished floor, not a large remodel of a room that was already a bathroom.

## What's included

**Typically included:**
- Mark and frame the bathroom corner
- Hang drywall and lay the vinyl floor
- Set the vanity, mirror, towel bar, and light
- Check the finished bath before you use it

**Customer prep:**
- Choose the corner before framing starts
- Clear stored items out of that bay

## How our Basement Bathroom Finishing process works

- **Bath layout** — The finisher marks the corner on the concrete, in the unfinished basement.
- **Bath wall** — That corner is framed. The rest of the basement stays unfinished.
- **Bath drywall** — Drywall goes on the new bathroom walls.
- **Bath floor** — Vinyl goes down. The vanity location stays open until the cabinet is set.
- **Set the vanity** — The vanity goes against the finished wall, with the mirror and light above it.
- **Inspect the bath** — The finisher checks the vanity, the light, and the floor.

## Materials and methods for Basement Bathroom Finishing

- **Vanity**: A simple vanity in the finished bathroom.
- **Mirror**: A plain mirror on the finished wall.
- **Tile**: A small field of wall tile beside the vanity.
- **Bath floor**: Vinyl, already down.
- **Towel bar**: Mounted on the painted wall.
- **Bath light**: A plain fixture above the mirror.
