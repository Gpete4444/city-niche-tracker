Built-in and custom cabinetry adds new cabinet units designed for a specific space — a kitchen island, mudroom, home office, or built-in pantry. Company Brand Name designs and installs custom cabinetry for Columbia homeowners who need storage that fits a space standard cabinets don't.

## What's Included With Built-In & Custom Cabinetry

- **Typically included:** design consultation, custom-built cabinet boxes and doors, and installation fitted to the space
- **Optional:** integrated lighting, matching trim and molding, specialty storage inserts
- **Customer prep:** confirming measurements and finalizing design before fabrication begins

## How Our Built-In & Custom Cabinetry Process Works

Step 1: Design consultation — discuss space, function, and style, same day.
Step 2: Measurements & drawings — precise measurements taken, custom drawings prepared, 2–3 days.
Step 3: Fabrication — cabinetry built off-site to spec, 2–3 weeks lead time.
Step 4: Site prep — installation area cleared and prepped, same day.
Step 5: Installation — cabinetry set, leveled, and secured, 1–2 days.
Step 6: Finish & hardware — trim, hardware, and touch-ups completed, 1 day.

Final cost depends on size, materials, and design complexity — [contact us](/contact) for a free estimate.

## Materials & Methods for Built-In & Custom Cabinetry

- **Plywood cabinet construction** — more dimensionally stable than particleboard in humid conditions
- **Catalyzed conversion varnish finish** — durable topcoat suited to Midlands humidity
- **Soft-close hinge and drawer hardware** — quiet, long-lasting function on custom units
- **Moisture-resistant sealant** — applied at seams to help resist swelling over time

## How long does a Built-In & Custom Cabinetry take?
Design consult — same day; Measurements & drawings — 2–3 days; Fabrication lead time — 2–3 weeks; Installation — 1–2 days; Finish & touch-ups — 1 day.

## Permits & Codes for Built-In & Custom Cabinetry in Columbia

### Usually needs a permit
- Built-ins that involve structural framing or wall changes
- Projects that relocate electrical for integrated lighting

### Usually no permit
- Free-standing or surface-mounted built-in cabinetry
- Cabinetry installed within an existing room footprint without structural changes

Confirm specifics with your city or county building department.

## DIY vs Hiring a Pro for Built-In & Custom Cabinetry

### DIY
- Possible for simple, small-scale built-ins using pre-made cabinet components
- Requires accurate measuring and secure wall anchoring

### Hire a pro
- Recommended for custom-sized cabinetry, structural framing, or integrated lighting
- Ensures proper leveling, secure fit, and code compliance on larger builds

- Want dedicated storage for a specific room or function
- Planning a kitchen island, mudroom, or home office upgrade
