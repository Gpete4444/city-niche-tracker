Built-in and custom cabinetry adds new storage or built-in features — pantry walls, mudroom lockers, window seats, entertainment centers — matched to the finish already used in the surrounding room. Company Brand Name provides built-in and custom cabinetry for Columbia homeowners adding function to a space rather than replacing what's already there.

Because these pieces are built to fit a specific space and finish, they're closer to custom carpentry than a standard cabinet swap, and pricing reflects that added design and fabrication work.

## What's Included With Built-In & Custom Cabinetry

- **Typically included:** design consultation, custom fabrication, on-site installation, finish matching to existing cabinetry
- **Optional:** integrated lighting, soft-close hardware, matching crown or trim details
- **Customer prep:** clearing the installation area and finalizing dimensions with the design team

## How Our Built-In & Custom Cabinetry Process Works

- **Consultation** — Design walkthrough covering the space, storage goals, and finish match
- **Measurement** — Precise measurements taken for a custom fit
- **Fabrication** — Cabinetry built off-site to the confirmed specifications
- **Delivery and setup** — Pieces are brought in and staged for install
- **Installation** — Cabinetry is set, leveled, and secured to the wall or floor
- **Finish matching** — On-site paint or stain work matched to existing cabinets
- **Cure** — Finish is left to cure fully before hardware goes on
- **Final walkthrough** — Fit, finish match, and hardware function checked

## Materials and Methods for Built-In & Custom Cabinetry

- **Stain-grade wood veneer** — Used when a built-in needs to match existing wood-tone cabinetry precisely, keeping grain and color consistent across old and new.
- **Sprayed cabinet-grade enamel** — Applied to painted-finish built-ins so the new piece matches the sheen and color of the rest of the kitchen exactly.
- **Conversion varnish topcoat** — Protects built-in surfaces from daily contact and moisture, an important detail for pieces near entryways or kitchens during humid Midlands summers.
- **Soft-close hinges and slides** — Standard on new built-in doors and drawers for quiet, long-lasting operation.
- **Cabinet-grade primer/sealer** — Applied to new wood surfaces before finish matching to ensure even color absorption.

## How Long Does a Built-In & Custom Cabinetry Take?

Custom fabrication adds lead time compared to refacing or refinishing an existing cabinet.

- **Design and measurement** — same day to a few days
- **Fabrication lead time** — 2–4 weeks
- **Installation** — 2–4 days
- **Finish matching and cure** — 2–3 days

## Permits and Codes for Built-In & Custom Cabinetry

### Usually needs a permit
- Built-ins that involve new electrical, such as integrated lighting
- Structural changes to walls or floors to accommodate a built-in

### Usually no permit
- Free-standing or wall-mounted cabinetry with no structural or electrical changes
- Finish matching and hardware on new built-ins

Confirm scope with your city or county building department before work begins.

## DIY vs Hiring a Pro for Built-In & Custom Cabinetry

### DIY
- Realistic for simple, small-scale shelving with basic carpentry skills
- Matching an existing cabinet finish exactly is difficult without spray equipment
- Structural anchoring for larger built-ins carries real risk if done incorrectly

### Hire a pro
- Custom fabrication ensures an exact fit for the space
- Finish matching blends smoothly with existing cabinetry
- Proper anchoring and code compliance handled as part of the project
