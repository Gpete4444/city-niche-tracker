Converting an old tub into a walk-in shower is one of the most popular bathroom upgrades — improving both daily accessibility and the overall look of the space. This service is a good fit for homeowners who rarely use their tub, want an easier-to-clean shower system, or are planning ahead for long-term accessibility.

## What's Included with Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions

This service includes removal of the existing tub, waterproofing, tile or surround installation, and new shower fixtures. Optional add-ons include built-in niches, bench seating, and frameless glass enclosures. We'll discuss accessibility needs and style preferences before finalizing the design.

## How Our Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions Process Works

Consult & Design — We assess your bathroom and discuss shower size, features, and style.
Material Selection — You choose tile, fixtures, and glass or curtain options.
Demolition & Installation — The old tub is removed, waterproofing applied, and the new shower installed.
Final Walkthrough — We confirm proper function and finish before completing the project.

## Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions Cost in Spanish Fork, UT

Cost depends on shower size, tile selection, and additional features like glass enclosures or built-in seating. Waterproofing quality is a critical (and non-negotiable) part of the cost — this isn't an area to cut corners. A free estimate provides an accurate range for your specific conversion.

## Materials & Methods for Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions

Proper waterproofing behind tile is the most important element of a durable shower conversion — it prevents moisture damage that can be costly to repair later. Tile and surround material selection affects both appearance and long-term maintenance, and slip-resistant flooring is an important consideration for safety.

## How Long Does Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions Take?

A typical walk-in shower conversion takes a couple of weeks, including demolition, waterproofing (which needs proper cure time), and tile installation. Custom glass enclosures may add time due to fabrication lead times.

## Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions FAQs

Will removing my tub affect resale value?
It can vary — some buyers prefer at least one tub in a home, particularly in a primary or secondary bathroom, while others prioritize walk-in showers. We can talk through what makes sense for your situation.

How important is waterproofing in a shower conversion?
It's essential — improper waterproofing is the leading cause of water damage in shower installations, so this step shouldn't be rushed or skipped.

Can I add accessibility features during the conversion?
Yes — features like grab bars, bench seating, and curbless entry can be incorporated into the design.

## DIY vs. Professional Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions

This is a project where professional installation matters most — improper waterproofing or tile work can lead to serious water damage down the line. DIY is not recommended for tub-to-shower conversions beyond cosmetic touches like fixtures.

## When to Call a Pro for Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions

Consider this conversion if you rarely use your tub, need improved accessibility, or your current shower/tub combo is showing signs of wear or water damage.

## Permits & Codes for Walk-In Shower & Tub Conversions

Shower conversions typically involve plumbing work and may require a permit and inspection. Verify specific requirements with Spanish Fork City or Utah County before starting your project.
