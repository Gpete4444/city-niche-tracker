Basement bedroom finishing turns an unfinished bay with a small window into a simple bedroom. The photos show the measurement, the new partition, the drywall, the paint, and the carpet. The window stays.

A legal bedroom in Elizabeth also needs an egress window and a permit. Elizabeth Basement Finishing Pros includes that in the scope when you want the room to count as a bedroom.

## What's included

**Typically included:**
- Measure the bay and frame the bedroom wall
- Hang and paint the drywall
- Fit the carpet, the door, the closet, and the window shade
- Check the paint line and the carpet

**Optional:**
- Egress window so the room can be a legal bedroom

**Customer prep:**
- Clear the bay
- Say whether the room has to pass as a legal bedroom

## How our Basement Bedroom Finishing process works

- **Bedroom layout** — A finisher measures the unfinished bay and the window.
- **Bedroom frame** — One partition is framed. The rest of the basement stays unfinished.
- **Bedroom drywall** — Drywall is hung on that wall.
- **Bedroom paint** — The new drywall is painted.
- **Bedroom carpet** — Carpet is fitted along the wall. The bed stays in the plan for the same spot.
- **Inspect the bedroom** — The finisher checks the paint and the carpet.

## Materials and methods for Basement Bedroom Finishing

- **Bedroom paint**: An even coat on the finished drywall.
- **Carpet**: The new bedroom floor.
- **Closet**: A finished closet with a rod.
- **Bedroom door**: A painted door in the hall.
- **Shade**: A plain shade on the original window.
- **Bedroom ceiling**: The finished ceiling with one light.
