Family room finishing turns an unfinished storage basement into a simple living room. The start is concrete and block. The result is drywall, a flat ceiling, vinyl plank, and a place to put a sofa.

Elizabeth Basement Finishing Pros measures the room, frames one wall where the layout needs it, hangs and tapes the drywall, and lays the floor. The window and the stair stay where they are.

## What's included

**Typically included:**
- Measure the unfinished room and mark the wall layout
- Frame and cover the new wall with drywall
- Tape, sand, and paint the joints
- Lay vinyl plank and set the baseboard

**Optional:**
- A second room off the same open basement
- A finished stair wall

**Customer prep:**
- Move stored boxes off the wall that will be framed
- Decide where the sofa and the window should stay

## How our Family Room Finishing process works

- **Measure the unfinished room** — A finisher tapes the concrete floor and the block walls before any lumber is cut.
- **Layout** — The new wall line is marked on the floor so the room stays the size you agreed to.
- **Frame one wall** — Studs go up on that line. The rest of the basement stays open.
- **Hang drywall** — One sheet at a time covers the new wall. The other walls can still be block.
- **Tape the joint** — Joints are taped and sanded so the wall reads as one surface.
- **Inspect the wall** — The finisher checks the paint, the baseboard, and the floor before the walkthrough.

## Materials and methods for Family Room Finishing

- **Drywall sheet**: Painted drywall is the finished wall. The framing stays behind it.
- **Joint compound**: The seam is sanded and painted so the sheet edges disappear.
- **Closed wall**: The studs are covered. The photo is the finished surface, not an open frame.
- **Vinyl plank**: The new floor runs to the baseboard where the concrete used to be.
- **Stair**: The basement stair stays, with a finished wall beside it.
- **Window**: The original basement window is trimmed into the finished wall.

## What does family room finishing cost in Elizabeth?
