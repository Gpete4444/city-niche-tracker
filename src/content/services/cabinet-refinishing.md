Cabinet refinishing updates the color and finish of existing cabinet doors, drawers, and boxes without replacing the cabinet structure. Company Brand Name handles the full process for Columbia homeowners — from sanding and priming through spraying a durable topcoat — so kitchens look new at a fraction of full-replacement cost.

## What's Included With Cabinet Refinishing

- **Typically included:** cabinet cleaning and degreasing, sanding for adhesion, primer application, and sprayed or hand-applied finish coats on doors, drawers, and boxes
- **Optional:** soft-close hinge upgrades, interior shelf refinishing, add-on drawer organizers
- **Customer prep:** clearing cabinet contents and countertop items before the crew arrives

## How Our Cabinet Refinishing Process Works

**1. Free consultation** — assess cabinet condition and select finish, same day.

**2. Cabinet prep & masking** — countertops and floors covered, same day.

**3. Door & drawer removal** — doors labeled and taken to the spray area, same day.

**4. Sanding & degreasing** — surfaces prepped for adhesion, 1 day.

**5. Primer application** — moisture-resistant primer applied, 1 day.

**6. Finish coats** — enamel or varnish sprayed in multiple coats, 1–2 days.

**7. Reinstall & hardware** — doors rehung, hardware attached, same day.

**8. Final walkthrough** — finish inspected together, touch-ups handled, same day.

## Materials & Methods for Cabinet Refinishing

- **Catalyzed conversion varnish** — hard, moisture-resistant clear coat suited to the Midlands' humid summers
- **Sprayed alkyd enamel** — smooth, brush-mark-free painted finish
- **Wood grain filler** — evens out oak grain before painting for a smoother result
- **Moisture-resistant primer** — helps prevent swelling and peeling in humid weather

## How Long Does a Cabinet Refinishing Take?

Consult — same day; Prep & masking — same day; Sanding & priming — 1–2 days; Finish coats & cure — 2–4 days; Reinstall & walkthrough — 1 day.

## DIY vs Hiring a Pro for Cabinet Refinishing

### DIY
- Workable for a single cabinet or small touch-up area
- Requires sanding tools, primer, and a ventilated space

### Hire a pro
- Recommended for whole-kitchen jobs where an even, factory-like finish matters
- Spray equipment and controlled cure conditions reduce brush marks and drips

## When to Call for Cabinet Refinishing

- Finish is peeling, chipping, or yellowing
- Cabinet color looks dated next to newer countertops or flooring
- Preparing to sell and want a quick, affordable kitchen update

## Cabinet Refinishing FAQs

What's the average cost to refinish cabinets?
Most Columbia-area cabinet refinishing projects run $2,500–$6,000 depending on kitchen size, with larger kitchens or islands running higher.

Is it cheaper to refinish or replace cabinets?
Refinishing is almost always cheaper than replacement since it reuses the existing cabinet boxes and only updates the finish.

How long does cabinet refinishing take?
Most projects take about a week from consultation to final walkthrough, with finish coats needing a few days to cure properly.

Can any cabinet be refinished?
Solid wood and many veneer cabinets refinish well. Heavily damaged or laminate cabinets may be better candidates for refacing instead.
