Cabinet hardware and hinge upgrades swap worn pulls, knobs, and hinges for new hardware, often paired with soft-close mechanisms. Company Brand Name offers this as a standalone service for Columbia homeowners who want a quick kitchen refresh without a full refinishing project.

## What's Included With Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades

- **Typically included:** removal of old hardware, installation of new pulls, knobs, or hinges, and hole alignment or drilling as needed
- **Optional:** soft-close drawer slide upgrades, matching cabinet hardware finishes throughout
- **Customer prep:** selecting hardware style and finish ahead of the visit

## How Our Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades Process Works

Step 1: Hardware selection — choose style and finish, same day.
Step 2: Old hardware removal — pulls, knobs, and hinges taken off, same day.
Step 3: Hole alignment — new holes drilled or existing ones reused, same day.
Step 4: New hardware install — pulls, knobs, and soft-close hinges attached, same day.

## Materials & Methods for Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades

- **Soft-close hinge hardware** — quieter, longer-lasting closing mechanism
- **Solid brass or stainless pulls** — corrosion-resistant finishes for humid kitchens
- **Soft-close drawer slides** — reduces slamming and wear on drawer boxes

## How Long Does a Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades Take?

Selection — same day; Removal & alignment — same day; Installation — same day; most jobs complete in a single visit.

## DIY vs Hiring a Pro for Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades

### DIY
- Very doable for homeowners with a drill and basic hand tools
- Works well when reusing existing hole patterns

### Hire a pro
- Worth it for new hole patterns across many doors, where alignment mistakes are hard to hide
- Saves time on a full-kitchen hardware overhaul

## When to Call for Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades

- Hinges are loose, squeaky, or no longer close softly
- Hardware finish looks dated or doesn't match newer fixtures

## Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades FAQs

How much does it cost to upgrade cabinet hardware?
Most hardware-only swaps run $300–$700 for a standard kitchen, with soft-close hinge upgrades adding $500–$1,000 depending on cabinet count.

Can hinges be upgraded without replacing doors?
Soft-close hinges typically install directly onto existing doors and boxes without any other cabinet work.

Is upgrading hardware a good DIY project?
It's one of the more DIY-friendly cabinet projects, especially when reusing existing hole patterns and basic tools.
