Cabinet hardware and hinge upgrades swap out pulls, knobs, and hinges — often including a switch to soft-close hardware — for one of the fastest, lowest-cost ways to modernize a kitchen. Company Brand Name provides cabinet hardware and hinge upgrades across Columbia both as a standalone service and as an add-on to refinishing or refacing.

It's also the fix of choice when doors and drawers no longer close flush due to worn or misaligned hinges.

## What's Included With Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades

- **Typically included:** removal of old hardware, hole filling or drilling as needed, new pull/knob/hinge installation, door and drawer alignment
- **Optional:** soft-close hinge or slide conversion, hardware finish upgrade (matte black, brushed brass, etc.)
- **Customer prep:** selecting hardware style ahead of the visit when possible

## How Our Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades Process Works

Consultation — Hardware style and placement reviewed with the homeowner
Door removal — Doors and drawers taken down for easier access
Hole prep — Existing holes filled or new ones drilled as needed
Alignment check — Hinges checked for warping or misalignment
Priming touch-up — Any exposed wood from hole changes primed
Installation — New pulls, knobs, and hinges installed
Adjustment — Hinges adjusted for even door reveals
Final walkthrough — Every door and drawer tested for smooth operation

## Materials and Methods for Cabinet Hardware & Hinge Upgrades

- **Soft-close hinges and slides** — The most requested upgrade, eliminating slamming doors and drawers and extending the life of both hardware and cabinet frames.
- **Cabinet-grade primer/sealer** — Used to touch up any bare wood exposed when hole positions change during a hardware upgrade.
- **Wood filler and epoxy repair** — Fills old hardware holes cleanly before new ones are drilled, avoiding a rough or uneven surface.
- **Corrosion-resistant hardware finishes** — Selected with the Midlands' humid climate in mind, since lower-grade metal finishes can dull or tarnish faster in a humid kitchen environment.
