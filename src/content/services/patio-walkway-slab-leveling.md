Patios and walkways settle unevenly after soil washout or freeze-thaw. Leveling restores a safer walking surface and keeps water from pooling against the foundation or house wall.

Foam injection works in tight side yards where heavy equipment for tear-out would damage landscaping. Most residential patio lifts finish in a few hours.
