Basement office finishing takes one unfinished corner and closes it into a room for a desk. The ceiling goes in under the joists, a light and a switch are set, and a closet door is hung on the new wall.

Elizabeth Basement Finishing Pros leaves the rest of the basement unfinished until you decide to finish it. The office is one room, not a gut of the whole floor.

## What's included

**Typically included:**
- Review the unfinished bay and mark the office
- Close the ceiling and set a recessed light
- Install the switch and outlet on the new drywall
- Hang the closet door and check the paint

**Customer prep:**
- Clear the corner where the desk will sit
- Say whether you want a closet on that wall

## How our Basement Office Finishing process works

- **Review the unfinished office bay** — The finisher looks at the block walls and the open joists before anything is closed.
- **Close the ceiling** — Ceiling panels go up under the joists. The walls can still be block at this step.
- **Set the light** — A recessed light is set into that new ceiling.
- **Wire the switch** — The switch and outlet go on the new drywall. The wall is not left open.
- **Hang the closet door** — The closet door is hung on the finished wall.
- **Inspect the office** — The finisher checks the light, the paint, and the door before you move the desk in.

## Materials and methods for Basement Office Finishing

- **Ceiling panel**: One panel of the finished ceiling, in place under the joists.
- **Recessed light**: The light trim in that ceiling.
- **Switch**: A switch plate on the new drywall.
- **Outlet**: A standard outlet above the baseboard.
- **Closet**: A simple closet built into the office.
- **Office light**: The ceiling light on, in the finished room.

## What does a basement office cost in Elizabeth?

Closing one corner is less than finishing the whole basement. Electrical for the new light and outlets is usually the line that moves the price.

- **Office bay** — $8,000–$16,000, ceiling, drywall, light, and a closet.
- **Office inside a full finish** — priced with the rest of the basement, usually $20,000–$45,000 for the whole footprint.
