Your primary bathroom sees daily use, which makes wear and dated finishes especially noticeable over time. This service focuses on remodeling primary suites — updating layout, fixtures, and finishes to create a space that functions well and feels like a retreat.

## What's Included with Primary Bathroom Remodels

Primary bathroom remodels typically include tile work, vanity and fixture replacement, and often a shower or tub upgrade. Larger projects may include layout changes to add double vanities or expand shower space. We'll discuss your priorities — whether that's more storage, a larger shower, or updated finishes.

## How Our Primary Bathroom Remodels Process Works

Consult & Design — We review your current primary bath and discuss layout and fixture goals.
Material Selection — You choose tile, vanity, and fixtures suited to a primary suite.
Demolition & Installation — Old fixtures and finishes are removed and replaced with new selections.
Final Walkthrough — We confirm everything is functioning and finished to your expectations.

## Primary Bathroom Remodels Cost in Spanish Fork, UT

Primary bathroom remodels tend to cost more than secondary bathrooms due to larger square footage and often more premium finishes. Cost drivers include tile selection, vanity size/quality, and whether the shower or tub is being reconfigured. A free estimate provides an accurate range for your specific project.

## Materials & Methods for Primary Bathroom Remodels

Tile, shower systems, and vanity materials are the key decisions in a primary bathroom remodel. Proper waterproofing is essential, especially for larger shower installations. We help select materials that balance durability and style for daily, long-term use.

## How Long Does Primary Bathroom Remodels Take?

Primary bathroom remodels typically take longer than secondary bathroom projects due to larger scope and often more custom finishes. Expect a longer timeline if layout changes or a double-vanity addition are involved.

## Primary Bathroom Remodels FAQs

Should I add a double vanity during my remodel?
It depends on your space and household needs — we can discuss whether your layout supports it.

What's the biggest cost factor in a primary bathroom remodel?
Tile and shower/tub selections typically drive cost the most, along with any layout changes.

How long will I be without my primary bathroom?
Most remodels take the bathroom fully offline; if it's your only full bath, we can discuss timing to minimize disruption.

## DIY vs. Professional Primary Bathroom Remodels

Cosmetic updates like paint and hardware are reasonable DIY tasks. Tile work, waterproofing, and plumbing changes should be handled by a professional to avoid costly water damage or code issues.

## When to Call a Pro for Primary Bathroom Remodels

Reach out if your primary bath feels outdated, storage is inadequate, tile or grout is failing, or you're preparing your home for sale.

## Permits & Codes for Primary Bathroom Remodels

Projects involving plumbing relocation or electrical work typically require permits. Verify specific requirements with Spanish Fork City or Utah County before starting.
