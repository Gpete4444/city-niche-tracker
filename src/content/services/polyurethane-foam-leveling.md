Foam leveling injects expanding polyurethane beneath settled concrete. The material fills voids, lifts the slab, and cures quickly—so you can often use the surface the same day.

It is lighter than mudjacking slurry, which matters on soils that already struggled under heavy fill. Ideal for driveways, garage aprons, and interior slabs where tear-out would disrupt the property.
