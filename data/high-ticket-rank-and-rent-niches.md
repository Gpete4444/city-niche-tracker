# The Ultimate Guide to Obscure, High-Ticket Niches for Local Lead Gen

> **Purpose:** This document catalogs **160+ obscure, high-ticket service niches and sub-niches** across 15 industry categories. Each niche was selected because it combines **high average job values** ($2,000+), **low local SEO competition**, and **genuine homeowner or business demand** — the three pillars of a profitable local lead gen site.

## How to Use This Guide

Every niche listed below represents a potential lead gen website. The **"Avg. Job Value"** column tells you what a single lead is worth to the contractor you rent the site to — the higher the number, the more you can charge for the site. The **"R\&R Opportunity Notes"** column explains *why* this niche is particularly suited to the local lead gen model.

| Tier | Avg. Job Value | What It Means for You |

## Strategy Notes for Course Students

**Choosing Your First Niche:** Start with a niche that has a minimum average job value of $5,000 and is relevant to your local market. For example, if you are in a coastal city, "hurricane window installation" is a natural fit. If you are in a cold-weather state, "basement finishing" or "geothermal heating" will have strong demand.

**Keyword Strategy:** The power of these niches is in long-tail, high-intent keywords. Instead of trying to rank for "plumber," you rank for "trenchless sewer line replacement \[city name\]" — a keyword that has far less competition but represents a $10,000+ job for the contractor.

**Finding Contractors to Rent To:** Once your site ranks, reach out to specialty contractors in that niche. They are often small businesses with 2–10 employees who do not have the time or knowledge to do SEO themselves. A steady stream of high-ticket leads is extremely valuable to them.

**Pricing Your Rent:** A common formula is to charge 10–15% of the average job value per lead generated. If your site generates 10 leads per month for a $15,000 service, that is $150,000 in potential revenue for the contractor — and $1,500–$2,250/month in rent for you.

**Scaling:** Once you have one site ranked and rented, replicate the process in a new city or a new niche. The niches in this guide give you over 160 options to choose from, ensuring you never run out of opportunities.
